# Makispace Support

Makispace finds space you can safely reclaim in your iPhone or iPad photo library: large videos, Live Photo clips, blurry shots, old screenshots, and near-duplicate bursts. Everything happens on your device.

## Contact

Email **[makispace.dev@gmail.com](mailto:makispace.dev@gmail.com)** with questions, bug reports, or ideas. You can also tap **⋯ → Send Feedback** in the app, which fills in your app version and device details for you. We aim to reply within a few days.

## Frequently asked questions

### I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days. Items you delete with Makispace go to the **Recently Deleted** album in the Photos app. Open **Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted**, select the items, and tap **Recover**.

### Does Makispace upload my photos?

No. Scanning, quality scoring, and compression all run on your device. Makispace has no servers, accounts, ads, or analytics. If a photo or video is stored only in iCloud, the Photos app may download it from Apple when you play, compress, or convert it. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) for details.

### Will compressing a video lose its date or location?

No. The compressed copy keeps the original's date, location, favorite status, and albums. The original goes to the Makispace trash, and nothing is deleted until you empty it and confirm.

### Why does a video say its preview is unavailable?

The video is stored only in iCloud. Makispace doesn't download large videos just to preview them, so it shows an estimated size instead. You can still compress it, and Makispace will download it at that point.

### Makispace isn't finding all my photos.

You may have given it access to only some photos. Go to **Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Makispace** and choose **Full Access**.

### How does the free allowance work?

You can free up to 500 MB without paying. Only space you actually free counts, at the moment you empty the trash. Reinstalling the app doesn't reset the allowance. Makispace Pro removes the limit.

### How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

In Makispace, tap **⋯ → Restore Purchases** and sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Tap **⋯ → Manage Subscription** in Makispace, or go to **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**. The 7-Day Pass doesn't renew, so there's nothing to cancel.

### How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at **[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)**.

## Requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or later, or iPad with iPadOS 18 or later.
