# Makispace Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-23

Makispace ("the app") is developed by Taryn Weiss ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it. In short: **Makispace analyzes your photo library entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or receive your photos or personal data.**

## Information the app accesses on your device

### Your photo library

With your permission, Makispace reads your photo library to find space you can reclaim. It looks at:

- Information about each photo and video, such as its file size, type, creation date, whether it is a Live Photo, screenshot, or favorite, and which albums it belongs to.
- Small, reduced-size versions of your photos, which Apple's on-device Vision framework analyzes to estimate image quality, detect "utility" shots such as receipts, and find visually similar photos.

All of this analysis runs on your device. Your photos and the results of the analysis are never sent to us or to any third party.

Makispace only changes your photo library when you ask it to:

- **Compressing a video** or **converting a Live Photo to a still** saves a new copy to your library, with the original's date, location, favorite status, and album membership.
- **Emptying the Makispace trash** asks iOS to delete the items you selected. iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted items move to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days.

If a photo or video is stored only in iCloud, Apple's Photos framework may download it from iCloud when you play, compress, or convert it. That transfer happens between your device and Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Makispace does not receive or relay that data.

### Information stored on your device

To avoid re-scanning your entire library each time, Makispace keeps the following on your device:

- **A library index** containing each item's identifier, size, date, type, and analysis results (quality scores and visual "fingerprints" used to group similar photos). It contains no photo or video content.
- **Your trash list**, meaning the items you have chosen to delete but haven't deleted yet.
- **A count of the storage freed with the free allowance.** This is stored in the iOS Keychain so the free allowance cannot be reset by reinstalling. It is a single number and contains no information about your photos.

The library index and trash list are deleted when you delete the app. Like other app data, they may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, depending on your settings. The Keychain count may remain on your device after the app is deleted, as is standard iOS behavior for Keychain items.

## Information we do not collect

Makispace has no user accounts, servers, advertising, analytics, or tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage data, or crash reports, and we do not sell or share personal information.

## Purchases

Purchases of Makispace Pro and the 7-Day Pass are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, name, or Apple Account information. The app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit framework on your device. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Feedback you send us

If you choose **Send Feedback**, Makispace opens your email app with a pre-filled message to [makispace.dev@gmail.com](mailto:makispace.dev@gmail.com). You review the message and decide whether to send it. If you leave **Include device info** turned on, the message also contains your app version, iOS version, device model, and the number of items Makispace has scanned. It never includes photos or other personal data.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond to you and to improve the app. We keep it no longer than necessary for those purposes, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

## Children

Makispace is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal data, it works the same for every user.

## Your choices and rights

- You can change or revoke Makispace's photo access at any time in **Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos**.
- You can delete all app data stored on your device by deleting the app.
- Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. The only personal information we may hold is feedback emails you have sent us. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and post the new version at this address. If the app ever begins collecting personal data, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required before doing so.
